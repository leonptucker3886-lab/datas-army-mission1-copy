# How to Connect Your Documents to Your After-Hours AI

The AI answers callers using the documents you upload. Keep them current and it gives accurate answers.

## What to upload
- Price list or service menu
- FAQs
- Policies: cancellations, warranties, payment, service area
- Hours, holidays and emergency rules

Formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT. One topic per file works best.

## Steps
1. Log in to your dashboard.
2. Open **Knowledge** > **Upload documents**.
3. Drag in your files, or click **Browse**.
4. Wait for each file to show **Ready**.
5. Click **Test a question**, ask something a caller would ask (for example, "How much is a drain cleaning?"), and check the answer.
6. Click **Publish**. The AI uses the new files on the next call.

## Updating
- To change prices, upload the new file and delete the old one. Don't keep two versions.
- Re-test after every change.

## Tips
- Write prices as plain numbers ("$149"), not images.
- Put urgent rules at the top: "If a caller reports a gas leak, tell them to call 911."
- Remove anything you don't want callers told.

## Resellers
Upload documents per client account. Switch clients from the account menu first, so each client's AI only uses its own files.

## What the business sends us
- [ ] Business name, address and main phone number
- [ ] Business hours, including holidays and closures (defines "after hours")
- [ ] Phone forwarding: forward the main line to our AI number after hours (we send instructions for your carrier)
- [ ] Greeting: how the AI should answer (name, tone, anything it must say)
- [ ] FAQs: top 10-20 questions callers ask, with approved answers
- [ ] Lead details to capture (name, callback number, reason for call, address, etc.)
- [ ] Where to send the morning summary: email address(es)
- [ ] Urgent-call rule: what counts as urgent and who to tell
- [ ] **Pro only:** documents for the AI to answer from (price lists, service menus, policies, PDFs)
- [ ] **Pro only:** mobile number(s) for SMS alerts
- [ ] **Pro only:** who needs dashboard logins (name + email)

## What the business gets back
**Starter**
- [ ] AI answers after-hours calls, takes messages and captures leads
- [ ] Email summary of every after-hours call by morning

**Pro** (everything in Starter, plus)
- [ ] Answers to caller questions from your own documents
- [ ] SMS alerts
- [ ] Dashboard with all calls and leads

## Not included on these plans
- Starter: no document answers, no SMS alerts, no dashboard (email summary only)
- Booking/escalation rules and priority support are Premium ($499/mo)
- White-label/reseller is Pro-only and sold separately (Partner/Agency tiers)

- [ ] We send a test call script; you place a test call after hours
- [ ] You confirm the summary arrived (email; plus SMS/dashboard on Pro)
